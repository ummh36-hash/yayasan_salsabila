## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a sanctuary of trust, spiritual warmth, and modern philanthropic stewardship. Targeted at contemporary Muslim donors, community leaders, and international partners, the interface balances institutional dignity with emotional hospitality. 

Drawing from modern organic minimalism and refined editorial craftsmanship, the aesthetic avoids dated or heavy ornamental tropes in favor of quiet sophistication. Layouts feel open, deliberate, and architectural. Subtle, vector-drawn eight-fold Islamic geometric rosettes and star tessellations are deployed sparingly—relegated to delicate watermark accents (4–6% opacity) or structural background framing. Physical surfaces convey weight and authenticity through warm, tactile paper tones rather than sterile clinical whites.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classical Islamic architectural gardens, natural foliage, and illuminated manuscripts:

- **Primary (`#0F5132` - Deep Emerald):** The core anchor for institutional headers, primary buttons, footers, and foundational statements. It reflects permanence, growth, and sacred legacy.
- **Secondary (`#15803D` - Vibrant Forest):** Employed for active states, confirmation accents, interactive badges, and progress indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C29B38` - Burnished Gold):** An intentional, restrained highlight hue reserved for urgent donation badges, campaign milestones, and decorative geometric rules.
- **Neutral Canvas & Tonal Steps:**
  - Base Background: `#F8FAF5` (Warm Alabaster Linen)
  - Surface Elevated: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright White)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F1F5E9` (Soft Sage Sand)
  - Surface Border: `#E2E8F0` / `#D4DEC9` (Muted Sand/Green Mist)
  - Text Primary: `#1C241E` (Deep Cypress Black, 14.2:1 contrast ratio against the base canvas)
  - Text Secondary: `#4A584E` (Muted Olive Charcoal)

## Typography

The type system pairs **Newsreader** (an authoritative, lyrical transitional serif) with **Plus Jakarta Sans** (a humanist, warm, and highly legible geometric sans-serif).

- **Editorial Serifs (Newsreader):** Used across H1 through H3 titles, Quranic/Hadith translation excerpts, and philosophical lead-ins. It introduces reverence and literary dignity.
- **Interface Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Used across operational components, metrics, data visualizations, campaign progress counters, forms, and general body reading. Its open counters ensure readability down to micro-labels.
- **RTL & Arabic Script Coexistence:** When paired with Arabic script (such as Amiri or Noto Naskh Arabic), Newsreader harmonizes with classical Naskh baselines, retaining proportional parity without visual clashing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fluid grid system configured to max-width containers of `1280px` for optimal reading lines and dashboard ergonomics.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` outer margins. Page sections enjoy generous vertical rhythm using `space-2xl` for emotional breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins. Two-column card lists compress to balanced vertical rhythms.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Donation panels and horizontal metrics reflow cleanly into full-width stacked configurations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, low-intensity ambient shadows combined with tonal borders to prevent digital harshness:

- **Surface Ground (`#F8FAF5`):** The default root canvas.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for interaction containers, article modules, and campaign cards.
- **Ambient Shadow System:** Shadows are tinted with a fraction of deep cypress (`rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.05)` to `rgba(28, 36, 30, 0.08)`) instead of synthetic neutral grey.
  - *Level 1 (Card Default):* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`, bounded by a `1px` continuous border in `#E2E8F0`.
  - *Level 2 (Hover / Floating Controls):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
  - *Level 3 (Modal / Donation Sticky Overlay):* `0 20px 32px -6px rgba(28, 36, 30, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced `0.5rem` (8px) baseline radius (`roundedness: 2`). This softened geometric contour reflects human warmth while remaining structured.

- Interactive controls (inputs, secondary buttons, campaign chips) utilize the standard `0.5rem` radius.
- Cards, modal containers, and hero banners scale to `rounded-lg` (`1rem`) and `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`).
- Micro-tags, category pills, and quick-donation tier presets leverage pill caps (`9999px`) to maintain clear interactive affordance against rectangular card backdrops.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0F5132`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold. Subtle hover transition to `#15803D` accompanied by a micro-elevation shift.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Border `1.5px solid #0F5132`, background transparent, text `#0F5132`. Active state fills to `#F1F5E9`.
- **Donation CTA Button:** High-prominence action item using `#0F5132` with a subtle top border bevel or tertiary accent badge (`#C29B38`) indicating tax-deductible or Zakat-eligible status.

### Donation Callout Cards
- **Container:** High-contrast structural element. Background in clean white (`#FFFFFF`) with a top accent trim of `4px solid #0F5132` or inverted dark theme (emerald `#0F5132` background with `#F8FAF5` text).
- **Amount Selectors:** Grid of toggleable pill pills (`$50`, `$100`, `$250`, `$500`, `Custom`). Active item marked by `#0F5132` background, white text, and an elevated shadow.
- **Campaign Metric:** Linear progress bar with `#F1F5E9` track and `#15803D` progress fill, accompanied by a numeric percentage readout.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Canvas-white background with a `1px` border of `#D4DEC9`.
- Focus state delivers a clear `2px` focus ring in `#0F5132` with `2px` offset, ensuring total keyboard accessibility.

### Cards & Content Modules
- Built using `#FFFFFF` surface with `1rem` corner rounding and subtle border `#E2E8F0`. 
- Header features dual layout: serif title above, category pill badge (`#F1F5E9` background, `#0F5132` text) positioned neatly at the top right.
- Subdued geometric vector patterns sit within header zones at 4% opacity, providing identity without distracting from textual legibility.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: `4px` radius, `#0F5132` check state with crisp white vector tick.
- Radios: Circular with inset `#0F5132` active dot on `#FFFFFF` track.
